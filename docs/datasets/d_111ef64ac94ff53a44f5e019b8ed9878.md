## Publisher description

This dataset provides the annual fresh cases handled by the State Courts' Court Dispute Resolution (CDR) cluster by case types, from 2015 to 2024. Notes: (1) Data is accurate as at 31 December 2024. (2) This dataset contains annual fresh cases handled by the State Courts' Court Dispute Resolution (CDR) cluster. The cluster provides court dispute resolution services for a wide range of matters in the State Courts, including civil cases and relational disputes such as harassment and neighbour disputes. (3) This dataset represents a subset of cases from the main State Courts caseload. (4) Writ of Summons is called Originating Claim under the Rules of Court 2021, with effect from 1 April 2022. Writ of Summons under Rules of Court 2014 can continue to be filed after 1 April 2022. (5) Originating Summons is called Originating Application under the Rules of Court 2021, with effect from 1 April 2022. Originating Summons under Rules of Court 2014 can continue to be filed after 1 April 2022.
