## Publisher description

Note: a. Data for 2021 is as of June 2021. b. Numbers may not add up to the totals due to rounding. c. The Fuel Mix presented in this table is calculated using the Output Method. The Output Method uses the amount of electricity generated and the corresponding type of fuel used to calculate the fuel mix for the generation of electricity. It takes into account the domestic fuel-to-electricity conversion efficiency of the generating plants and the plant technology. d. From 2016, the category "Others" includes solar.
