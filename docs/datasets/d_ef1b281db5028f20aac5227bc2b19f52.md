## Publisher description

Source: ENERGY MARKET AUTHORITY Footnotes: Data refer to electricity access on mainland Singapore. Planning regions in Singapore, namely Central, West, North, North-East and East Regions, are based on Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) Master Plan 2019 (www.ura.gov.sg/Corporate/Planning/Master-Plan/Master-Plan-2019). Data are used for SDG 7.1.1 'Proportion of population with access to electricity'. Please refer to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Global Metadata (https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata) for more information on SDG 7.1.1.
