## Publisher description

Marked Historic Sites are places of historical significance, for e.g., important event or personality are being commemorated at the site to serve as reminders of our history. It has a blue plaque / heritage marker which consists of a write-up on the history of the site. They are marked by the National Heritage Board. For some of these sites, the building may not be around anymore, as they are not gazetted buildings, unlike national monuments.
